A Letter

I didn't go looking for you.

I don't think I needed to.

You just arrived — and suddenly everything made sense.

I've seen you laugh until you forget yourself.

I've seen you be someone's whole world.

I've seen you soft, and sure, and stunning.

And I think about it —

how even before I knew you,

you were somewhere out there

already being this remarkable.

You don't need me to tell you how beautiful you are.

But I will anyway.

Every single day.

— Abel
